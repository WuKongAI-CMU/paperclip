# DearMe Product Launch Video Brief - Future Concept

Date: 2026-05-13
Owner: DearMe product / launch narrative
Use: prompt pack for video-generation tools, launch page copy, founder demo, and investor/customer teasers.
Status: future concept film. Do not present public sending, public deployment, ad spend, or outreach as live automatic behavior unless the user explicitly approves it in the product story.

## Core Thesis

DearMe is not a writing tool. DearMe is a private brand team for one person.

The user gives one sentence: what they want to be known for. DearMe studies their work, voice, proof, and relationships, then turns that private reality into public momentum: drafts, opportunities, a personal site, launch decisions, and a daily letter that tells them what their future self is building.

The product promise:

> Your future public self, already working.

## Master Video Prompt

Use this as the main prompt for a video-generation AI:

```text
Create a cinematic near-future product launch film for "DearMe", a private AI brand team for one person.

The film follows one ambitious person over a single morning. They are a founder, creator, consultant, or senior operator with a messy private world: notes, calls, code, past work, voice memos, relationships, unfinished drafts, and proof scattered everywhere. They say one sentence into their phone: "I want to be known for turning complicated work into public trust."

DearMe quietly begins working. Do not show robots. Do not show a generic chatbot. Show a private team presence through elegant, human, calm interface moments: a living identity map, voice fingerprint, proof cards, warm opportunity notes, private draft packets, a personal site becoming ready on a phone, and a launch decision waiting for approval.

Visual style: premium, intimate, human, cinematic, near-future but believable. Warm daylight, glass, paper, phone screens, subtle motion, soft shadows, high-end documentary pacing. Interfaces should feel useful and alive, not like a sci-fi hologram. The product should feel like an invisible chief of staff, strategist, writer, researcher, site builder, and opportunity scout working behind the user.

Story arc:
1. The user has talent but no public surface.
2. They give DearMe one sentence about what they want to be known for.
3. DearMe builds a private identity dossier from their real work.
4. DearMe prepares proof-backed content, opportunities, and a personal site.
5. DearMe pauses at one public launch call: "Approve this move?"
6. The user approves.
7. Their public self becomes visible: a site, a post, a warm intro, and a daily Dear me letter.

On-screen text should be minimal and premium:
"One sentence in."
"A private team starts working."
"Your voice. Your proof. Your opportunities."
"Nothing public without your call."
"DearMe."
"Your future public self, already working."

No robots, no cartoon avatars, no crowded dashboards, no crypto visuals, no generic SaaS stock footage, no fake analytics explosions, no dark hacker room, no visible code, no internal technical terms.
```

## 90-Second Launch Film

### 0-8s - The Problem

Visual:
Morning. A person looks at a laptop filled with scattered proof: a half-written post, a calendar invite, a GitHub/activity page, a client note, a podcast clip, a resume, a folder of old work. Nothing looks broken; it looks underused.

On-screen text:
`You are already becoming known for something.`

Voiceover:
> 你做了很多真正有价值的事。问题是，世界看不见。

### 8-16s - One Sentence

Visual:
The person opens DearMe on their phone. One clean start. One clean input.

On-screen input:
`I want to be known for turning complicated work into public trust.`

On-screen text:
`One sentence in.`

Voiceover:
> DearMe 从一个句子开始：你想因为什么被记住。

### 16-30s - The Private Team Wakes Up

Visual:
Instead of a chatbot bubble, show a calm private working surface forming around the person: proof cards, voice lines, audience map, opportunity notes. Human silhouettes may appear briefly as reflections or soft team-like motion, but not as literal robot characters.

On-screen text:
`A private brand team starts working.`

Voiceover:
> 它不是替你写几段文案。它像一个只服务于你的品牌团队，先理解你的声音、你的证据、你的关系和你的野心。

### 30-46s - Proof Becomes Assets

Visual:
Private work transforms into useful launch assets:

- a voice profile locks in
- three proof-backed post drafts appear
- five warm opportunity leads organize themselves
- a personal site preview opens on a phone
- a daily letter card appears

On-screen text:
`Voice -> Proof -> Opportunity -> Site`

Voiceover:
> 你的经历变成证据。证据变成内容。内容连接机会。机会反过来让你的名字更清楚。

### 46-60s - Future Product Moment

Visual:
A near-future "future self" layer appears: not fantasy, not avatar-heavy. The user sees a timeline of possible public selves:

- `Known for AI product strategy`
- `Known for technical leadership`
- `Known for creator-led consulting`

DearMe highlights the path with the most proof and the lowest trust risk.

On-screen text:
`Your future self needs a system.`

Voiceover:
> DearMe 不只是帮你今天发什么。它帮你选择未来一年，你要被谁发现、因为什么被信任。

### 60-74s - Approval Boundary

Visual:
One strong decision card appears:

`Ready to launch`

Prepared work:

- publish the proof-backed post
- send one warm intro
- update the private site preview

Button:
`Approve`

On-screen text:
`Nothing public without your call.`

Voiceover:
> 它可以一直做私下准备。但真正公开的动作，永远等你决定。

### 74-84s - Public Momentum

Visual:
The user taps approve. A tasteful sequence shows a post going live, a personal site on a phone, a warm reply arriving, and the Dear me letter summarizing the day. Keep it grounded and premium.

On-screen text:
`You did the work. DearMe made it visible.`

Voiceover:
> 你做的工作没有变少。只是第一次，有一个系统持续把它变成机会。

### 84-90s - End Card

Visual:
Minimal white or warm off-white end card. DearMe wordmark. One sentence.

On-screen text:
`DearMe`

Tagline:
`Your future public self, already working.`

Voiceover:
> DearMe。你的未来公众身份，已经开始工作。

## Internal Reuse Rationale

This video concept absorbs the strongest product mechanisms from the competitive research without exposing donor or substrate language:

- Polsia: one input -> immediate private work -> visible receipts -> manager-style launch call.
- Lindy: compact action-card boundary where the system prepares work and the owner approves consequential actions.
- Littlebird: dense, readable task rows and proof cards instead of a vague chatbot transcript.
- DearMe current architecture: private proof, voice fit, opportunities, personal site, daily letter, and public-action approval remain the customer-visible product spine.

The film should make autonomy feel useful, not risky: DearMe keeps working privately, but the public threshold is a clean user call.
